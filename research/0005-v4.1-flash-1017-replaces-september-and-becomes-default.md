# 0005 — V4.1-Flash-1017 replaces the September checkpoint and becomes the default

- Date: 2026-10-18
- Status: accepted. Supersedes 0004's "keep the September entry beside it".

## Context

0004 added `deepinfra:deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017` beside the September
checkpoint. The same day Lachlan made two calls: the new checkpoint **replaces** the
September one, and it becomes **the default model in every app** that pins the
catalogue.

The evidence behind them, beyond 0004's probe:

- **A first-call ambiguity screen on wandly's live prompt**, 5 samples each. The
  question was ambiguous in the words but settled by the prompt's context. Measured
  from the start of the call to the first tool call:

  | Model | First-call reasoning, median | Seconds, median |
  |---|---|---|
  | MiMo-V2.6-Pro | 1,201 chars | 17.5 |
  | GLM-5.3 (DeepInfra, `max`) | 2,031 chars | 14.7 |
  | V4.1-Flash-1017 (`high`) | 248 chars | 1.8 |

  Read by hand, MiMo argued with itself over the reading the context had ruled out in
  4 of 5 samples, and once planned an extra query to settle it. GLM-5.3 did so once,
  briefly, and 1017 did so once, in a clause. The prompt and the reasoning stay in
  wandly; only these measurements are copied here.
- **Price:** $0.098 / $0.294 per Mtok with DeepInfra's promotion, against MiMo's
  $0.43 / $0.87 and GLM-5.3's $0.5625 / $2.50.

## Decision

1. The September entry `deepinfra:deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash` leaves the
   catalogue. Every app that used it moves to 1017 in the same lock bump, because an
   app that still names the old key fails at import once it bumps.
2. In each app, the **assistant default** and **every use of the September
   checkpoint** move to 1017. Batch jobs chosen on their own measurements stay as they
   are: legal's ingest enrichment and NZ match check, and moving-house's non-assistant
   `MODEL`.
3. **Wandly and legal switch their defaults only after their own quality harnesses**
   have run on 1017: wandly's agent-quality probe and legal's marking eval. On an
   earlier cheap DeepSeek checkpoint, wandly 0129's harness caught a correctness fault
   that the speed and price numbers above cannot see. Chat, fitness and moving-house
   have no such harness, and switch now.

## Why

1017 measured as well as or better than its predecessor on every fact the catalogue
records. It is the fastest and cheapest model in the menu, and on the one behaviour
screened it was the most direct. Keeping two checkpoints of one model means two
ladders to maintain for no app that wants the older one.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep both (0004).** Superseded: no app wanted the September checkpoint once 1017
  was measured.
- **Switch every default with no evals.** The screen above measures how quickly a
  model settles what a question means, not whether its answer is right. The two apps
  that can measure answers do so first.

## Consequences

- The 0.51 discount is a promotion with no end date (0004). Once it ends, 1017 costs
  $0.20 / $0.60, which is still the cheapest in every menu.
- Unset effort is **off** on 1017, as on every DeepSeek checkpoint here. An app that
  wants it to think must send a level; sending nothing gets no reasoning.
- If wandly's or legal's harness says 1017 falls short, that app keeps its current
  default, and this note gets a line saying so.
