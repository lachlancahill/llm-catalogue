# 0007 — GPT-6.1 Sol: the real window, and replay measured

- Date: 2026-10-23
- Status: accepted

## Context

Wandly added `openai:gpt-6.1-sol` to its Ask menu (wandly 0191). Its thread ceiling is
the smallest `context_tokens` on its menu times three bytes, less 1 MB of room for a turn,
so the 272,000 recorded here as a floor would have made that ceiling negative and refused
every turn on every model.

## Decision

- **`context_tokens` is 922,000**, the model page's "max input tokens" (1,050,000 context,
  128,000 output). The 272,000 floor came from reading the long-context price tier as the
  window. An oversized request returns `context_length_exceeded` without naming a limit,
  so the page is the source, dated in `manual.py`.
- **Measured on `/v1/responses`**, one sample each: `none` and `minimal` are a 400 naming
  the five supported values; `low` through `max` each return a real function call; unset
  echoes back as `medium`. The ladder stays the documented one.
- **`preserves_replayed_reasoning=True`.** Encrypted reasoning items sent back with
  `store=False` are counted as input: 398 prompt tokens without call 1's 371 reasoning
  tokens, 771 with them; on the next user turn 464 against 971. The response reports
  `reasoning.context='all_turns'`, so earlier turns' reasoning is read too, not dropped.

## Consequences

- Legal takes the larger window on its next bump of this pin.
- Longest silence in a `max` stream, 3,506 reasoning tokens: 10.2 s. Recorded in wandly
  0191, where it is checked against that app's 90-second read timeout.
